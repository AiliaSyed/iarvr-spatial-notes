## More than a final result

This site documents my progress in **Interaction in Virtual and Augmented Reality (IARVR)** at TU Darmstadt. The goal is to show the process behind each task rather than only the final result.

## How I will write entries

Each entry will capture the **goal**, **approach**, **experiments**, **results** and **reflection**. When useful, I will include screenshots, code snippets, sketches, videos and links to source code.

## Technology

The website is built with **Hugo**, written in Markdown, versioned with Git and designed for publication on **GitHub Pages**. A commit history helps track the evolution of the documentation.

## Sections

- **Labs:** Practical tasks and technical lessons
- **Reverse Classroom:** Research, presentation and reflection
- **Final Project:** Dual-Path Spellcasting from concept to evaluation

## Next steps

Replace planning notes with actual results as the tasks progress, link relevant commits and keep the documentation updated throughout the semester.
