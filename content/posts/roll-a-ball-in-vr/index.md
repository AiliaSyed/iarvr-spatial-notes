## Assignment

According to Lab 02, begin with *Roll-a-Ball*: move a ball through a scene, collect pick-ups and increase the score. Then adapt the interaction to virtual reality on the Quest.

## Planned implementation

The VR version introduces a small board in the scene that can be grabbed and tilted using the XR interaction system. The ball moves through physics, while the interface displays pick-up count and completion feedback.

## Technical points

- Unity Rigidbody and Collider components
- XR Grab interactions
- World-space UI and TextMeshPro
- Building for Android / Meta Quest
